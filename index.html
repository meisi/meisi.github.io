03 / ABOUT

Connecting computation
and human experience.

My research brings together artificial intelligence, cognitive science, and interactive media. I work on embodied conversational agents, computational narrative, and virtual environments, with applications in education and human–AI collaboration.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California and an M.A. in Psychology from the University of Cincinnati.
